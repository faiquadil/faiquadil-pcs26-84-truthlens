PROJECT INFORMATION

About TruthLens AI

An academic project exploring machine learning and deep learning for fake news detection.

PCS26/84

Deep Learning-Based
Fake News Detection

Explore how text classification models can identify patterns associated with fake and real news labels in a research dataset.

01 / OBJECTIVE

Project objective

Build and evaluate text-based classification models and provide an accessible interface for analysing news articles.

02 / APPROACH

Methodology

Dataset inspection, preprocessing, leakage-aware splitting, model training, evaluation and explainability.

03 / MODELS

Models explored

Logistic Regression, Linear SVM, LSTM, BiLSTM and a DistilBERT pilot experiment.

04 / TECHNOLOGY

Technology stack

Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, PyTorch, FastAPI, HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Research limitation: A classifier learns associations from its training dataset. A Real prediction does not prove a story is true, and a Fake prediction does not independently prove it false. Always verify claims using reliable independent sources.
